## Full description (plain text, < 16,000 chars)
```
VidGrab finds the video on the page you are watching and saves it to your computer.

HOW IT WORKS
- Open a page with a video and click the VidGrab icon. The extension lists the videos the page plays (MP4 and HLS streams) with quality and length, so you can pick one.
- Files are downloaded by your browser first. HLS streams (.m3u8) are fetched in parallel and joined into a single MP4 inside your browser.
- VidGrab's server is used only to count your daily allowance, and as a fallback when the browser cannot get the file by itself.
- DASH streams (.mpd) can be handed to the VidGrab Windows app, if you have it installed on the same computer.

FEATURES
- Detect the video a page plays (MP4, HLS, DASH) on supported sites, and on any site once you choose to allow it.
- Download in the browser, with resume of interrupted downloads.
- Channel scan: open a channel or profile on Douyin, Facebook, TikTok or Instagram, scan with a Stop button, tick the videos you want in a list and press "download N videos to this computer".
- Video-on-screen detection on feeds: the video you are looking at is picked up while you scroll.
- Preview before download: see the file and its size before you save it.
- QR code: send a video link to your phone.
- File name template: choose how saved files are named.
- Recording mode (last resort): when a player gives no file, you can press "Quay tab" to record the tab as a WebM file. It starts only when you press the button.
- Account sync: sign in on the VidGrab website to sync your allowance and history. The extension keeps its own 30-day session, which you can sign out of at any time.
- Right-click menu and notification when a download finishes.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
- It does not download DRM-protected or encrypted streams. They are shown as unsupported.
- It does not download live streams. They are clearly marked.
- It does not bypass any protection.
- It does not download from YouTube. In this version, YouTube links are not handled.

YOUR CONTENT, YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
Download only content that you own, that is in the public domain or licensed for download, or that you otherwise have the right to save. You are responsible for respecting the copyright and the terms of use of each site.

PRIVACY
- The list of videos found on a tab stays on your device and is deleted with the tab.
- Page addresses are sent to VidGrab's server only for actions that need it (counting your daily allowance, server fallback, channel following if you ask for it).
- For sites that require sign-in, the extension can send that site's cookies to VidGrab's own server for that single extraction; the server uses them once and deletes them.
- Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.
Privacy policy: https://dvid.vibe1.tinhgon.xyz/en/privacy

PERMISSIONS
Access to all sites is optional. It is never granted at install; the popup asks for it (Chrome shows its own prompt) and you can revoke it on chrome://extensions.

Supported sites include TikTok, Douyin, Facebook, Instagram, X, Reddit, Threads, Pinterest, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, Snapchat, VK, Twitch, Rumble, Odysee, Dailymotion and SoundCloud. Availability depends on each site and may change.
```
